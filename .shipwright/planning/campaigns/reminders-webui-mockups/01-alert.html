1. The alert: where does "something is due" show up?

Counts reminders and parked triage items whose day has come, for the selected project (or all projects).

CLI call: uv run --python ">=3.11" --no-project reminder_cli.py due --json (read-only, run from the project's main checkout)

Option A: header badgerecommended, with C

BoardTriageSettings
Due 3

Always visible, calm, one click opens the due list. Easy to overlook, because it is small.

Option B: banner on top of the board

3 things are due today - 2 reminders, 1 parked triage item. Open list

Hard to miss, same orange as the "behind origin" notice. Can get annoying if you cannot act right away.

Option C: entry in the Inboxrecommended, with A

reminder Renew the signing certificate
due today - open the due list
triage Revisit: flaky Windows runner
park expired 4 days ago

Lives where you already look for things that need you. Does not shout.

Recommendation: A + C. The badge tells you something is there; the Inbox entries are the list. Use the banner (B) only when an item is overdue by more than a day. A parked triage item stays in the alert every day until you act on it (that is how the CLI works); a snoozed reminder reappears on its snooze date.
