Embedded terminal (macOS): come back from sleep and it revives the moment you click or type, instead of staying frozen ~30-60 seconds or needing a browser reload. On a Mac the page keeps running through a lock/sleep but the connection to the host dies silently, and macOS fires none of the browser signals the earlier fixes relied on — so recovery had fallen back to a slow timer. The terminal now treats your first interaction as "I'm back, check the connection" and reconnects at once, and it also notices a dead connection roughly three times faster on its own. (FR-01.28)
