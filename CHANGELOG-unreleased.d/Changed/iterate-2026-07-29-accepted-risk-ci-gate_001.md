Corrected the recorded explanation of what keeps the `github-actions-mutable-action-tag` finding quiet. Nothing in CI suppresses it - `security.yml` runs a bare Semgrep scan that reads no `SHIPWRIGHT_SEMGREP_*` variable, and the finding simply never blocks because the critical gate fires only on CVSS-critical findings or leaked secrets. Moving the toggle into the workflow would not be a harmless tidy-up: the new register gate would discover it as an unrecorded suppression and fail the build.
